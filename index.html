Questions people ask

What does Daya Dua Daan Foundation do?

We run 26 programmes across India in education and skilling, health and wellbeing, worker welfare, animal care and environment, assisted pilgrimage and cultural exchange, disaster relief and dignity in last rites.

Is my donation tax deductible?

Once our approval under Section 80G of the Income-tax Act 1961 is issued, Indian taxpayers can claim a deduction, subject to the limits in the Act. You receive a receipt and Form 10BE for every donation.

Does the foundation work all over India?

Yes. We work across India rather than in a single state, with programmes chosen for each region according to local need.

Can I choose which programme my donation supports?

Yes. Select any of the 26 programmes when you donate and the amount is held in a separate ledger for that work alone.
