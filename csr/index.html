CSR partnerships

We are set up to receive corporate social responsibility funds under Section 135 of the Companies Act 2013, and to report against them the way a board and its auditors actually need.

Which of our programmes qualify

Almost all of them map to Schedule VII. Education, skilling and livelihood enhancement; healthcare and sanitation; women's empowerment and old age homes; environmental sustainability, water conservation and animal welfare; protection of national heritage, art and culture; disaster management including relief and rehabilitation; and rural development. Our programme pages set out the cost structure of each.

What you receive

  • A written project proposal with scope, budget, timeline and measurable outcomes
  • Our CSR-1 registration number and registration certificates
  • Funds held in a separate project ledger, never pooled
  • Quarterly progress reports and a utilisation certificate
  • Impact assessment where the CSR Rules require one
  • Site visits for your team, at any time and without notice

Projects that suit corporate funding well

Construction and infrastructure companies. Water recycling from your own dewatering operations, safety equipment and skill certification for mathadi and construction workers, and disaster-resilient building programmes.

Technology companies. AI and IT training centres in district towns, digital literacy for senior citizens, and cyber-fraud awareness.

Healthcare and pharmaceutical companies. Screening camps, assistive devices, blood and platelet donor networks, and mental health counselling.

Consumer and retail companies. Artisan livelihoods, women's self-help group market linkage, and menstrual health in schools.

Getting started

Write to csr@dayaduadaan.com with the Schedule VII areas your board has approved and your budget range. We respond with a costed proposal within a week, and we will tell you plainly if we think another organisation is better placed to deliver it.
