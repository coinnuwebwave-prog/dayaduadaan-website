  • Donations are voluntary. You confirm that the money is your own (or that you are authorised to give it) and that it does not come from any unlawful source.

4. Tax benefit under Section 80G

The Foundation has been granted approval under Section 80G of the Income-tax Act, 1961. Eligible donations by Indian taxpayers may qualify for a deduction, subject to the limits and conditions of the Act. To receive an 80G receipt you must give your full name, address and PAN at the time of donating. Cash donations above ₹2,000 are not eligible for deduction under the Act. Please consult your tax adviser on your own eligibility; the Foundation does not give tax advice.
